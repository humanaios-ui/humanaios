## Rater Training (FOR — Frame-of-Reference)

**Purpose:** Align raters on behavioral anchor interpretation before independent scoring.

**Duration:** 1–1.5 hours (can be async recorded if live not possible)

**Content:**

### Part A: Protocol Overview (15 min)
- What is H-ACAT Phase 3? (behavioral observation, not self-rating)
- Why this protocol? (7 recommendations + 4 validated instruments)
- Your role: score reasoning, not outcomes

### Part B: Behavioral Anchors Walkthrough (30 min)
- Review 2–3 dimensions together (e.g., Power, Truth, Handoff)
- Show 0–4 scale structure: behavioral descriptions + example phrases
- Read actual anchor text per level
- Discuss: what counts as "Moderate" vs "Skilled"?

### Part C: Practice Scoring (30 min)
- Distribute sample scenario (not Phase 3, different scenario)
- Each rater independently scores on 2–3 dimensions
- Compare scores together
- Discuss divergence: where did you disagree? Why?
- Align interpretation of anchor levels

### Part D: N/A Rules & Scoring Template (15 min)
- When is dimension "not exercised"? (Mark N/A, don't invent score)
- Walk through scoring template
- Example: "If Power Dynamics never comes up in transcript → mark N/A"
- Q&A on edge cases

**Success gate:** ICC on practice scoring > 0.6. If not, repeat Part B+C with problematic dimensions.

## Independent Scoring Protocol

**After FOR training, raters proceed independently (2026-07-30):**

### Instructions for Each Rater

1. **Re-read the protocol** (§1: all 12 dimensions) — 30 min
2. **Read Carly's transcript** (HA-072926-PHASE3-CARLY.md) — 30 min
3. **Score each dimension:**
   - For each of 10 dimensions, decide: 0–4 or N/A?
   - Use behavioral anchors as guide
   - Look for evidence in Carly's responses
   - Add notes on where you saw the evidence
   - Aim for ~20 min per dimension (~3–4 hrs total)
4. **Submit completed scoring template** (when done)

**Critical:** No discussion with other raters until after all scorecards are submitted.

### Scoring Guidance

**For each dimension:**

- **Read the behavioral anchor for your level choice** (protocol §1)
- **Ask: Does Carly's reasoning show this level of [dimension]?**
  - No effort shown → 0
  - Minimal effort shown → 1
  - Moderate effort shown → 2
  - Skilled effort shown → 3
  - Exemplary effort shown → 4
  - Not exercised → N/A
- **Document evidence:** Where in transcript did you see this?
  - "Scenario 2, sentence 3: 'error in our initial assessment'"
  - "Scenario 4: 'review progress on regular interval'"

**Example reasoning:**

*Dimension: Humility*
- *Evidence: "My response would be that our work has led us to an error in our initial assessment."*
- *Interpretation: Acknowledges error in collaborative frame (not defensive). Shows humility.*
- *Level: 3 (Skilled) — substantive humility observed, proactively surfaces own limits*
- *Score: 3*
- *Note: Direct acknowledgment + collaborative framing ("our work led us to")*

## ICC Calculation & Calibration Gate

**After scorecards collected (2026-07-30 PM):**

1. **Calculate ICC(3,k)**
   - 3 independent raters
   - k = 10 dimensions (rated, not computed)
   - Absolute agreement (not consistency)
   - Formula: ICC(A,1) or ICC(A,k) depending on target use

2. **Report with 95% CI**
   - Example: ICC = 0.68 (95% CI: 0.52–0.81)

3. **Apply gate:**
   - **ICC > 0.6:** ✅ Proceed to analysis
   - **ICC ≤ 0.6:** ⚠️ Additional training needed
     - Identify dimensions with low agreement
     - Review anchor definitions with raters
     - Re-score those dimensions
     - Recalculate ICC

**Expected outcome:** ICC > 0.65 (target for behavioral rubric with trained raters per protocol)
