## Decision 002: Biweekly Publication Cadence (not Weekly)

**What:** Publish Seed Constitution posts biweekly (every other Tuesday), not weekly

**Who:** Carly R. Anderson

**Why:**
- Weekly is the statistical sweet spot for growth (evidence: Substack research)
- But solo operator sustainability matters more (consistency > heroics)
- Biweekly is maintainable long-term + still signals commitment
- Quality > volume (time to think between posts)

**Grounded in Principles:**
- **Principle 8** (Service Orientation) — avoid burnout that degrades service
- **Principle 9** (Open Process) — the cadence decision is published

**Trade-off:**
- Growth might be slower than weekly
- Sustainability + quality likely higher

**Status:** ✅ DECIDED

**Next review:** Nov 26, 2026 (reassess after 8 posts)

## How to Propose a Governance Decision

Want to propose a change to the Constitution? Here's the process:

1. **Open an issue** with label `governance-proposal`
   - Describe: What decision needs making?
   - Why: Why does it matter?
   - Grounded in which Principle(s)?
   - Evidence: What informs this proposal?

2. **Carly reviews** (weekly, usually Friday)
   - Requests clarification or evidence if needed
   - Decides: adopt, defer, decline
   - Logs decision + rationale here

3. **Community discusses**
   - Questions are welcome (thread in GitHub issue)
   - Not a vote (humans have authority, Principle 1)
   - But Carly reads + considers feedback

4. **Decision logged** (this file)
   - Links to issue + discussion
   - Evidence summarized
   - Next review date set

**Timeline:** 1–2 weeks from proposal to decision + logging.
