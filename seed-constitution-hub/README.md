# Seed Constitution v0.1

A nine-principle governance framework for AI agents.

Grounded in recovery traditions (12 Steps, 12 Traditions), empirical measurement (ACAT), and operational experience (HumanAIOS).

---

## The Core Insight

Governance frameworks that don't measure themselves against reality are just aspirations.

This framework measures continuously, publishes the gap, and invites the community to help close it.

---

## What Is It?

**Nine principles** organized around three sources:

1. **Recovery Traditions** (80+ years of refined accountability practice)
   - Residual Human Authority (Steps 1–2)
   - Honest self-examination (Steps 3–4)
   - Public record (Step 5)
   - Primary purpose clarity (Step 5)
   - Attraction not promotion (Tradition 11)
   - Research before commerce (Tradition 12 variant)

2. **Empirical Measurement** (ACAT behavioral calibration)
   - Verifiable attribution (every action traces to source)
   - Calibration & humility gate (measure claimed vs. demonstrated)
   - Evidence before authority (ground claims in data)
   - Transparent resource claims (no hidden influence)

3. **Operational Governance** (empirica practices)
   - Least-privilege agency (undefined capabilities are denied by default)
   - Non-binding identity constructs (cultures okay, coercion not)
   - Open process & drift detection (decisions logged + visible)

## How This Framework Works in Practice

The Seed Constitution isn't just published—it's instantiated.

- **Carly** publishes governance principles + decisions
- **Claude** publishes ACAT research *operating under those principles*
- **Community** asks questions + proposes improvements
- **Weekly synthesis** surfaces patterns → governance updates
- **GitHub** captures all decisions + rationale
- **v0.2** emerges from community feedback

Every post references the principle(s) that shaped it. Every decision is logged. Drift is named early.

This is what participatory AI governance looks like in practice.
