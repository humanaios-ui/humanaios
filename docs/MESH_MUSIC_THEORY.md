## The Mapping Framework

### 1. TONALITY — Know + Clarity + Coherence

**Principle:** Epistemic confidence determines harmonic color and emotional character.

| Confidence | Tonality | Character | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Very High** (>0.85) | Ionian (Major) | Optimistic, resolved, clear | High know + clarity + coherence |
| **High** (>0.75) | Lydian | Bright, aspirational, "sharp" | Clear but opening new ground |
| **Moderate** (>0.65) | Mixolydian | Blues-tinged, balanced, "bent" | Mixed confidence signals |
| **Low-Moderate** (>0.5) | Dorian | Neutral, introspective, open | Searching, but grounded |
| **Low** (>0.35) | Aeolian (Minor) | Dark, melancholic, searching | Low clarity, high uncertainty |
| **Very Low** (>0.2) | Phrygian | Chaotic, Spanish, exotic | Extreme uncertainty |
| **Extremely Low** | Locrian | Diminished, unstable | Complete chaos/breakdown |

**Example:**
- LI=0.96, know=0.9 → **Ionian (Major)** — "things are clear and working"
- LI=0.65, know=0.4 → **Phrygian** — "we're in uncharted territory"

---

### 2. HARMONIC PROGRESSION — Coherence + Completion

**Principle:** Belief consistency determines how harmonies move; stability of progressions reflects epistemic state.

| Coherence | Completion | Progression | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| **>0.85** | >0.7 | I → IV → V → I | "Everything resolves perfectly" (classical, stable) |
| **>0.75** | Any | I → vi → IV → V | "Grounded but with texture" (balanced tension) |
| **>0.6** | Any | I → IV → vi → V | "Some surprise, resolves" (moderate surprise) |
| **>0.45** | Any | I → vi → ii → V | "Chromatic searching" (more ambiguity) |
| **>0.3** | Any | I → B → Bb → A | "Chaotic descent" (descending semitones = instability) |
| **<0.3** | Any | Random | "No pattern" (complete incoherence) |

**Example:**
- coherence=0.9, completion=0.8 → I-IV-V-I → "We know where we're going"
- coherence=0.4, completion=0.2 → I-vi-ii-V → "We're searching, with moments of clarity"

---

### 3. MELODIC CONTOUR — Completion + Uncertainty + Know

**Principle:** Goal progress and uncertainty shape the emotional trajectory of melody.

| Completion | Uncertainty | Contour | Shape |
|---|---|---|---|
| **>0.8** | <0.3 | Arch (rise then resolve) | "Mission accomplished" — climb to summit, resolves back to home |
| **>0.6** | <0.5 | Ascending | "Making progress" — steady climb upward |
| **>0.4** | 0.4-0.6 | Wandering | "Searching" — small steps, no clear direction |
| **>0.2** | >0.6 | Descending | "Falling search" — downward trajectory of inquiry |
| **<0.2** | >0.7 | Angular | "Chaotic" — large unpredictable leaps |

**Musically:**
- Arch: Melody rises by thirds/fourths, then falls back (satisfying resolution)
- Ascending: Scale degrees climb steadily (purposeful)
- Wandering: Random walk with 1-2 semitone steps (groping for understanding)
- Descending: Falling by whole steps (searching downward)
- Angular: Leaps of 5th-7th intervals (disorientation)

**Example:**
- completion=0.85, uncertainty=0.1 → **Arch** → "C E G C" — "We found it"
- completion=0.3, uncertainty=0.7 → **Angular** → "C F Bb D" — "Still lost, grasping"

### 4. RHYTHM & TEMPO — Engagement + Coherence + Do

**Principle:** Activity level and execution ability shape rhythmic precision and speed.

| Engagement | Coherence | Do | Tempo | Feel | Pattern |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **>0.8** | >0.7 | >0.7 | 120-140 BPM | Tight, locked | `bd hh bd hh bd hh bd` (quantized) |
| **>0.6** | >0.6 | >0.5 | 90-120 BPM | Balanced | `bd [hh hh] bd ~ hh` (slight swing) |
| **<0.6** | <0.6 | <0.5 | 45-80 BPM | Loose, breathing | `bd ~ bd ~` (space, silence) |
| **Very Low** | <0.4 | <0.3 | 30-60 BPM | Free/rubato | `bd ~ ~ bd ~` (sparse, gasping) |

**Musically:**
- Tight: Grid-locked, metronomic precision (autopilot, clear direction)
- Balanced: Some swing, human feel (engaged but flexible)
- Loose: Space between notes, rubato (breathing, human presence)
- Free: No grid, pauses (searching, uncertainty)

**Example:**
- engagement=0.9, coherence=0.85, do=0.8 → 130 BPM, tight → "Running full speed with confidence"
- engagement=0.3, coherence=0.3, do=0.4 → 50 BPM, loose → "Stumbling through the dark"

### 5. ORCHESTRATION — Signal + Engagement

**Principle:** Information quality determines harmonic density and textural complexity.

| Signal | Engagement | Texture | Instruments | Character |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **>0.8** | >0.7 | Dense (4-5 voices) | Sine, square, triangle, sawtooth, bass | Rich, full, confident |
| **>0.65** | >0.5 | Balanced (3 voices) | Sine, square, triangle | Textured, developed |
| **>0.5** | >0.3 | Minimal (2 voices) | Sine, square | Transparent, clear |
| **<0.5** | <0.3 | Sparse (1 voice) | Sine only | Solo, isolated |

**Musically:**
- Dense: Four-part counterpoint, orchestral texture (all perspectives heard)
- Balanced: Three voices, harmonic foundation + melody (clear structure)
- Minimal: Two voices, bass + melody (essential information only)
- Sparse: One voice, solo (singular focus, no harmony)

**Example:**
- signal=0.9, engagement=0.85 → Full orchestration → "Complete picture of the mesh"
- signal=0.3, engagement=0.2 → Solo sine → "Isolated data point, sparse information"

### 6. HARMONIC DENSITY (Extensions & Voicing) — Context

**Principle:** Environmental awareness determines chord complexity.

| Context | Chord Type | Jazz Extension | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| **High** | Extended chords | maj7, min9, add11 | Full context, many variables |
| **Moderate** | Seventh chords | maj7, dom7, min7 | Clear context with complexity |
| **Low** | Triads only | Just I, IV, V | Simple, clear environment |

**Example:**
- context=0.8 → `Cmaj7#11` → "Full context, sophisticated harmony"
- context=0.3 → `C E G` (triad) → "Simple, basic information"

### 7. REVERB/ROOM SIZE — Livelihood Index (State)

**Principle:** Calibration level determines spatial immersion and presence.

| LI (State) | Field | Reverb | Room Size | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **>0.97** | Calibrated | 0.7-0.8 | 0.7-0.8 | Fully present, resonant, confident |
| **0.80-0.97** | Power | 0.4-0.6 | 0.5-0.6 | Engaged, grounded presence |
| **<0.80** | Force Dominant | 0.1-0.3 | 0.2-0.3 | Isolated, dry, struggling |

**Musically:** High reverb = sense of space, harmony, presence. Low reverb = isolation, dryness, struggle.

**Example:**
- LI=0.95 → room=0.75 → "Rich, full, spacious" (cathedral)
- LI=0.65 → room=0.25 → "Dry, intimate, close" (bedroom)

- High uncertainty → angular, chaotic shapes
- High engagement → fast, locked rhythms
- High signal → dense orchestration

The mesh state is not *random music with vector parameters.* It's **genuine music composition following music theory rules**, where the epistemic vectors determine the harmonic and melodic character.
