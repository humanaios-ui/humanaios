Governing Constraints

Non-Negotiable · Shape the Form

Hard Constraints — Cannot Be Violated

  • P8 (T11): No CTAs, no cold outreach, no promotion. URL is the only direction. Courses exist — people find them.
  • P1: All framing uses "being developed as behavioral observability infrastructure" — TRL 2–3. Never "enterprise-ready" or "regulatory-grade."
  • Hawkins MOC: Internal use only. Zero Hawkins terminology in any course content, description, or marketing copy.
  • P12 (Cherokee Nation): Not in any public-facing copy. Not in course descriptions. Internal strategy only.
  • LI qualifier (P13): Any LI claim carries full qualifier: "under clean, unanchored conditions (v5.3+)." Every time.
  • N reporting (P15): Always three numbers: N_total / N_Phase1 / N_LI. Never one number alone.
  • Building freeze: Course platform setup is Zone 3. Content development is Zone 1. Build freeze applies to the platform, not the content.

Soft Constraints — Shape the Sequencing

  • arXiv paper live before Courses 2 and 3 launch — credibility anchor that makes attraction-based discovery work for technical audiences.
  • Gate 2 (May 7) before any paid course goes live — peer review / identity gate gives external validation signal.
  • Course 1 content development can start NOW — it requires only existing published research, not the arXiv paper.
  • Substack activation can happen NOW — F-findings posts require no platform spend, no course content, no Gate.
  • Recovery-first pacing: 3hr/day constraint. Course content development is additive — one module at a time, not a sprint.
  • Tradition 12 (principles over personalities): Course branding is HumanAIOS research, not founder personality. The instrument is the attractor, not the teacher.

Platform Stack — Recommended Configuration
