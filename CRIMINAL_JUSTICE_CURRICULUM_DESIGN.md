# Criminal Justice Recovery Curriculum — Complete Design (T6)

**Level 50-200 reentry recovery pathway aligned to 7-step framework + Lozoff principles**

**Status:** T6 Design Phase  
**Grounding:** 30+ years NIJ research + Restorative Justice + Bo Lozoff framework  
**Timeline:** 6-month program (can extend to 12-18 months for deeper work)

---

## Curriculum Philosophy

**Core principle:** "We're all doing time" — everyone is imprisoned by something.

Reentry isn't about punishment/release. It's about **transformation from confinement (literal and psychological) to freedom (material and spiritual).**

This curriculum teaches:
1. **Individual recovery** (personal transformation)
2. **Systemic recovery** (justice system transformation)
3. **Community recovery** (relationships + reintegration)
4. **Economic recovery** (employment + stability)
5. **Spiritual recovery** (meaning-making + purpose)

All through the same 7-step pattern.

## Course Structure (Level 50-200)

### **TIER 1: FOUNDATION (LEVELS 50-100)** — First 8 weeks

#### **Course J1.1: "We're All Doing Time" (Level 50-75)**
**Duration:** 2 weeks | **Frequency:** Daily + 2x weekly groups  
**Prerequisite:** None (can be taken standalone or as entry to reentry track)

**Learning Objectives:**
- Recognize confinement (what imprisons you?)
- Understand recursivity (we all face limitations)
- Admit: "I need help; I'm powerless alone"

**Curriculum:**
- **Week 1: Recognition Phase**
  - Day 1-3: Read Bo Lozoff's "We're All Doing Time" excerpts
  - Day 4-7: Meditation practice (10 min daily, guided by Lozoff's method)
  - Reflection: "What confines me? (Addiction? Incarceration? Fear? Trauma?)"
  
- **Week 2: Admission Phase**
  - Daily: Meditation + journaling (what do I need to admit?)
  - Group: Share your confinement (peer witness model)
  - Homework: Write your "admission statement"
  - Reflection: "Who have I harmed by my confinement?"

**Teaching Method:**
- Lozoff texts + video (meditation teaching)
- Peer circles (restorative justice format)
- Daily journaling
- Guest speaker: Someone who recovered

**Success Metrics:**
- ✓ Student completes meditation practice (12+ sessions)
- ✓ Student writes admission statement
- ✓ Student participates in peer circle (shares honestly)
- ✓ Student can name: "I'm confined by [X]; I need help"

**Assessment:** Peer feedback + self-reflection (no grades; all pass who engage)

**Materials:** Lozoff texts, meditation guides, journaling prompts, peer circle facilitation guide

---

#### **Course J1.2: "Why People Get Out" (Level 75-100)**
**Duration:** 3 weeks | **Frequency:** 3x weekly + daily optional  
**Prerequisite:** J1.1 or life experience in reentry

**Learning Objectives:**
- See reentry is possible (Belief phase)
- Understand success factors (what actually works?)
- Form hope (recovery is real)

**Curriculum:**
- **Week 1: See Success**
  - Video: 5 reentry success stories (people 3-5 years out, stable)
  - Guest: Person who successfully reintegrated (life testimony)
  - Discussion: "What made the difference for them?"
  - Reflection: "Which success model resonates with me?"
  
- **Week 2: Understand the Path**
  - Module: Employment pathways (what jobs work for reentry?)
  - Module: Housing options (stable housing is foundation)
  - Module: Family reconnection (rebuild relationships)
  - Module: Peer support (mentorship networks)
  - Reflection: "Which pathway fits my goals?"
  
- **Week 3: Form Your Goal**
  - Peer coaching: "What do you want your life to look like in 5 years?"
  - Group: Share goals with accountability partners
  - Meditation: Visualization (imagine yourself recovered)
  - Reflection: "Do I believe this is possible for me?"

**Teaching Method:**
- Video testimony (reentry success stories)
- Module-based learning (each factor of success)
- Peer coaching + accountability partnerships
- Guided meditation + visualization

**Success Metrics:**
- ✓ Student identifies 3+ success factors
- ✓ Student can explain why reentry works (specific reasons)
- ✓ Student forms a 5-year goal (written)
- ✓ Student says: "I believe recovery is possible"

**Assessment:** Goal statement + peer feedback

**Materials:** Video testimonials, employment/housing resource guides, peer coaching worksheets

---

#### **Course J1.3: "The Reentry Map" (Level 75-100, continues to 100-150)**
**Duration:** 4 weeks | **Frequency:** 2x weekly + self-paced  
**Prerequisite:** None

**Learning Objectives:**
- Understand reentry journey (phases, timelines, expectations)
- Map your resources (who/what supports you?)
- Prepare for transition (practical readiness)

**Curriculum:**
- **Week 1: The Journey**
  - Module: Pre-release (what to do before release? 3-6 months)
  - Module: First 30 days (highest-risk window; what's needed?)
  - Module: Months 2-6 (stabilization phase; employment, housing)
  - Module: Year 1 (rebuilding relationships; finding meaning)
  - Module: Years 2-5 (deep integration; helping others)
  - Reflection: "Where am I in this journey?"
  
- **Week 2: Your Resources**
  - Worksheet: Map your support network (family, mentors, programs)
  - Worksheet: Identify gaps (what's missing? How to fill it?)
  - Worksheet: Build accountability (who will hold you to your goals?)
  - Group: Share your resource map (get feedback)
  
- **Week 3: Practical Prep**
  - If pre-release: How to prepare (education, skills, mindset)
  - If newly released: First-month survival (housing, employment, crisis management)
  - If stabilizing: How to deepen (community, relationships, purpose)
  - Reflection: "What's my next 90-day goal?"
  
- **Week 4: Integration**
  - Peer circle: "What have I learned about my journey?"
  - Commitment: "I commit to [specific goal] in the next 90 days"
  - Meditation: Grounding (root yourself in your strength)
  - Celebration: Acknowledge progress so far

**Teaching Method:**
- Roadmap visualization (see your journey)
- Practical worksheets (map your resources)
- Peer circles (share your stage + get support)
- Guided meditation (grounding practice)

**Success Metrics:**
- ✓ Student completes resource map (identifies support network)
- ✓ Student articulates: "I'm at [stage] in reentry journey"
- ✓ Student forms 90-day goal
- ✓ Student identifies accountability partner

**Assessment:** Resource map + 90-day goal statement

**Materials:** Reentry roadmap, resource worksheets, accountability partner agreement

### **TIER 2: ACTIVE RECOVERY (LEVELS 100-150)** — Next 12 weeks

#### **Course J2.1: "Commitment & Discipline" (Level 100-125)**
**Duration:** 4 weeks | **Frequency:** 3x weekly  
**Prerequisite:** Tier 1 courses or pre-release status

**Learning Objectives:**
- Commit to reentry discipline
- Build consistent practice (employment, mentoring, meditation)
- Measure progress (daily/weekly/monthly wins)

**Curriculum:**
- **Week 1: Choose Your Discipline**
  - Options: Employment track, education track, mentorship track, or combination
  - Reflection: "What discipline will I practice?"
  - Goal-setting: SMART goals for next 90 days
  
- **Weeks 2-4: Practice**
  - Daily: Meditation (20 min, Lozoff's compassion practice)
  - 3x weekly: Group check-ins (accountability)
  - Weekly: Track progress (job applications, hours worked, mentoring, education)
  - Reflection: Weekly journaling on discipline + growth

**Teaching Method:**
- Goal-setting workshops
- Daily meditation (group + individual)
- Accountability groups (weekly check-ins)
- Progress tracking (measurable metrics)
- Guest: Someone who practiced consistently (testimony)

**Success Metrics:**
- ✓ Student completes daily meditation (60+ sessions)
- ✓ Student reaches discipline milestones (employment interviews, mentoring hours, education progress)
- ✓ Student maintains accountability group attendance (80%+)
- ✓ Student reports: "Consistency is paying off"

**Assessment:** Progress tracking + peer accountability

**Materials:** Meditation guides, goal-setting templates, accountability group facilitator guide

---

#### **Course J2.2: "Audit & Accountability" (Level 125-150)**
**Duration:** 4 weeks | **Frequency:** 2x weekly + group  
**Prerequisite:** J1.1-1.3 + 8+ weeks reentry experience

**Learning Objectives:**
- Audit harm caused (Inventory phase)
- Understand victims' impact (empathy practice)
- Prepare for amends (if applicable: restitution, apology, changed behavior)

**Curriculum:**
- **Week 1: Audit Your Impact**
  - Worksheet: "Who did my crime/confinement harm?"
  - Worksheet: "What was the impact? (Economic, emotional, relational, safety)"
  - Worksheet: "What have I already changed?"
  - Reflection: Honest assessment (no minimization)
  
- **Week 2: Victim Perspective**
  - Video: Victim impact statements (listening without defending)
  - Module: Understanding trauma (how crime affects victims)
  - Group: Discuss (compassion practice for victims)
  - Reflection: "What does victim healing look like?"
  
- **Week 3: Restorative Justice**
  - If applicable: Prepare for victim-offender dialogue (facilitated, optional)
  - If not applicable: Plan indirect amends (community service, changed behavior)
  - Commitment: "How will I make amends?"
  - Reflection: "What does accountability feel like?"
  
- **Week 4: Integration**
  - Peer circle: Share your inventory + amends plan
  - Accountability: Commit publicly to your amends
  - Meditation: Compassion practice (toward victims + yourself)

**Teaching Method:**
- Impact audit worksheets
- Video testimony (victim perspectives)
- Restorative justice facilitation (if applicable)
- Peer circles (shame-free accountability)
- Compassion meditation

**Success Metrics:**
- ✓ Student completes honest impact audit
- ✓ Student engages with victim perspective (watches, reflects, discusses)
- ✓ Student forms amends plan (restitution, changed behavior, community service)
- ✓ Student reports: "I understand the impact of my actions"

**Assessment:** Impact audit + amends plan + peer reflection

**Materials:** Impact audit templates, victim impact videos, restorative justice facilitation guide

---

#### **Course J2.3: "Making It Right" (Level 150-175)**
**Duration:** 4 weeks | **Frequency:** 2x weekly  
**Prerequisite:** J2.2 completed

**Learning Objectives:**
- Implement amends (Amends phase)
- Rebuild relationships (family, community)
- Demonstrate changed behavior (consistency)

**Curriculum:**
- **Week 1: Restitution & Service**
  - Action: Begin restitution payment plan (if applicable)
  - Action: Start community service or peer mentoring
  - Tracking: Document amends progress
  - Reflection: "What does change feel like?"
  
- **Week 2: Family Repair**
  - Module: How to rebuild trust (honesty, consistency, time)
  - Mediated conversation: Family meeting (if ready/safe)
  - Reflection: "What relationships matter most? What needs repair?"
  - Action: Commit to specific actions (calls, visits, amends)
  
- **Week 3: Community Contribution**
  - Action: Volunteer work or mentoring (deepening)
  - Reflection: "How am I giving back?"
  - Celebration: Acknowledge contributions (peer recognition)
  - Meditation: Gratitude practice (for those who helped + those you serve)
  
- **Week 4: Witness & Celebrate**
  - Peer circle: Share amends progress
  - Recognition: Celebrate transformation (certificates, celebrations)
  - Commitment: "I'm rebuilding; I'm contributing"
  - Forward look: "What comes next?"

**Teaching Method:**
- Action-oriented (actually doing amends, not just planning)
- Peer witness (accountability + celebration)
- Family mediation (if appropriate)
- Gratitude meditation (grounding in recovery)

**Success Metrics:**
- ✓ Student is actively making restitution/community service
- ✓ Student has initiated family repair (at least one conversation)
- ✓ Student is mentoring or serving others
- ✓ Student reports: "People see I've changed"

**Assessment:** Amends tracking + peer recognition + family feedback

**Materials:** Restitution tracking, family mediation guides, volunteer coordination

---

### **TIER 3: INTEGRATION & SERVICE (LEVELS 175-200)** — Final 8 weeks

#### **Course J3.1: "Full Circle" (Level 175-200)**
**Duration:** 4 weeks | **Frequency:** 1x weekly + ongoing  
**Prerequisite:** 5+ years post-release OR 12+ weeks reentry + stable housing/employment

**Learning Objectives:**
- Achieve integrated reentry (stable, meaningful, integrated)
- Mentor others (service phase)
- Advocate for systemic change (if interested)

**Curriculum:**
- **Week 1: Assess Integration**
  - Reflection: "Where am I now vs. where I started?"
  - Metrics: Employment, housing, relationships, mental health, legal status, meaning
  - Celebration: What's working? What shifted?
  - Acknowledgment: Recognize your transformation
  
- **Week 2: Mentor & Teach**
  - Role: Become peer mentor for new reentry participants
  - Training: How to mentor (share your experience without fixing others)
  - Practice: Mentoring sessions (1-on-1 or group)
  - Reflection: "What wisdom do I have to share?"
  
- **Week 3: Systemic Awareness**
  - Module: How injustice operates (mass incarceration, disparate impact)
  - Option: Advocacy training (if interested in policy work)
  - Option: Community organizing (if interested in grassroots change)
  - Reflection: "How do I want to contribute to change?"
  
- **Week 4: Ongoing Commitment**
  - Group: "Full Circle" celebration (recognize integration milestone)
  - Commitment: "I will continue my recovery + help others"
  - Meditation: Purpose practice (meaning + legacy)
  - Vision: "What does my legacy look like?"

**Teaching Method:**
- Reflection + celebration (honoring transformation)
- Peer mentorship (teaching others)
- Advocacy/organizing training (optional systemic work)
- Purpose meditation (meaning-making)

**Success Metrics:**
- ✓ Student has stable employment/income
- ✓ Student has stable housing
- ✓ Student has rebuilt key relationships
- ✓ Student is mentoring others
- ✓ Student has defined their contribution (mentoring, advocacy, community service, other)
- ✓ Student reports: "I'm integrated; I'm at peace"

**Assessment:** Integration interview + mentorship evaluation

**Materials:** Mentorship training, advocacy resources, purpose reflection guide

## Assessment & Completion

**Student completes track when:**
- ✓ Passes all 7 courses (engagement-based, not grade-based)
- ✓ Completes required hours (meditation, groups, accountability)
- ✓ Demonstrates competency (can explain 7-step pattern)
- ✓ Shows integration (stable housing, employment, relationships, meaning)

**Certificate:** "Recovery Practitioner - Criminal Justice Track"
- Recognition of transformation
- Readiness to mentor others
- Permission to take advanced courses (advocacy, community organizing)

## Success Metrics (Institutional)

**Measure track effectiveness:**

**Individual Level:**
- Recidivism at 1 year: <20% (vs. 60% national average)
- Employment at 6 months: >70%
- Housing stability at 6 months: >80%
- Family reconnection at 1 year: >75%
- Reports life satisfaction: >7/10

**Systemic Level:**
- Partners with 10+ reentry organizations
- Reaches 1,000+ participants in Year 1
- Advocacy wins (policy changes, funding, system reform)
- Mentor pipeline (10% of graduates become mentors)
