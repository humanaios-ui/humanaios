## FRAMEWORK 2: RESOURCE CONSTRAINTS ANALYSIS (Theory of Constraints Applied)

**Question:** What resource bottleneck is limiting your growth most?

### Constraint Identification (Current State)

**Applying ToC: Identify → Exploit → Subordinate → Elevate → Repeat**

**Primary Constraint (Bottleneck): CAPITAL/FUNDING**

**Evidence:**
- Operating budget unknown (presumed limited)
- No funding streams active (grants not submitted, investors not engaged, revenue $0)
- Growth limited by capacity to hire + scale infrastructure
- If you had $2M today, what would you do? (Bottleneck answer = you'd immediately invest it)

**Cascade Impact:**

```
CAPITAL BOTTLENECK
    ↓ (limits hiring, infrastructure)
PEOPLE CAPACITY BOTTLENECK
    ↓ (Night overloaded, practices understaffed)
RESEARCH OUTPUT BOTTLENECK
    ↓ (can't hire researchers, can't do enough ACAT assessments)
REVENUE BOTTLENECK
    ↓ (can't monetize data, consulting, licenses without products)
GROWTH BOTTLENECK
    ↓ (stuck at small scale)
```

**Secondary Constraint: NIGHT'S PERSONAL CAPACITY**

**Evidence:**
- Night is expertise bottleneck (evaluation methodology, system design, strategic direction)
- Night can't be cloned or easily replaced
- Night's time is dividing across 13 practices + Resource Miner + fundraising
- Every decision, strategic call, complex problem routes back to Night

**Cascade Impact:**

```
NIGHT'S CAPACITY BOTTLENECK
    ↓ (Night overloaded)
DECISION-MAKING SPEED BOTTLENECK
    ↓ (bottleneck for Admiral authority, strategy decisions)
PRACTICE AUTONOMY BOTTLENECK
    ↓ (practices waiting on Night for guidance, escalations)
SCALING BOTTLENECK
    ↓ (can't scale without more leadership)
```

### ToC Cycle: Exploit, Subordinate, Elevate

**For CAPITAL BOTTLENECK:**

1. **Identify:** ✅ Capital is limiting (primary constraint)

2. **Exploit:** Get maximum value from capital BEFORE getting more
   - Which projects give highest ROI?
   - Which Resource Miner cycles are highest-impact?
   - Current state: Unknown (not measured)
   - Action: **Define CAPITAL ROI metrics** → measure ROI per $1 invested

3. **Subordinate:** Pace other work to capital availability
   - Don't hire ahead of funding (don't burn cash on speculation)
   - Prioritize: Which practices should scale first? Which should wait?
   - Current state: Ambiguous (no prioritization defined)
   - Action: **Define capital allocation hierarchy** → which practices scale first?

4. **Elevate:** Increase capital
   - Resource Miner focused on: Grants → VC → Angels → Revenue
   - Timeline: $500K by Q4 2026, $2M by Q2 2027
   - Current state: Not active (needs Resource Miner staffing + strategy)
   - Action: **Activate Resource Miner immediately** → start grant/investor pipeline

5. **Repeat:** Once capital is less constrained, what's the next bottleneck?
   - Likely: People capacity (hiring, onboarding, culture scaling)
   - Or: Night's personal capacity (need deputy CEO or co-leadership)

**For NIGHT'S CAPACITY BOTTLENECK:**

1. **Identify:** ✅ Night is expertise + decision bottleneck

2. **Exploit:** Get maximum value from Night's time BEFORE cloning/replacing
   - Focus Night on: Highest-leverage decisions (strategy, Resource Miner oversight, investor relations)
   - Delegate to: Strategic Lead (autonomy) for execution, Operations Steward (mesh-support) for coordination
   - Current state: Night is in all decisions (distributed, not focused)
   - Action: **Create decision authority matrix** → what must Night decide vs. what can practices decide?

3. **Subordinate:** Pace organizational decisions to Night's availability
   - Don't flood Night with decisions
   - Queue decisions: Urgent (same day), Important (by week-end), Routine (by month-end)
   - Current state: Unknown (no queue/prioritization)
   - Action: **Define decision queue** → what's urgent vs. routine?

4. **Elevate:** Increase Night's capacity
   - Hire/deputize: Strategic Lead role needs strengthening (autonomy lead becomes CEO equivalent)
   - Hire/deputize: Operations Steward needs strategic authority (not just coordinator)
   - Create: C-suite meeting cadence (Strategic Lead, Steward, Weaver + Custodian) to handle strategic decisions without Night for routine items
   - Timeline: Build deputy leadership by Q1 2027

5. **Repeat:** Once Night's capacity is less constrained (via deputies), what's the next bottleneck?
   - Likely: Research output (hard to produce ACAT assessments at scale)
   - Or: User adoption (if HumanAIOS launches, need to scale user base)
