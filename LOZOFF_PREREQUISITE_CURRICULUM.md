# "We're All Doing Time" — Prerequisite Course (T8)

**Bo Lozoff Foundation: All students take this before choosing domain track**

**Status:** T8 Course Design  
**Duration:** 2 weeks intensive OR 4 weeks self-paced  
**Level:** 50 (Foundation — no prerequisites)  
**Required:** Yes, before accessing any domain track  
**Teaching Method:** Video + daily practice + peer circles + reflection

---

## Course Philosophy

**"We're all doing time" — Bo Lozoff's universal principle**

Everyone is imprisoned by something:
- Addiction
- Bias (internal or external)
- Depleted resources
- Trauma
- Systemic injustice
- Lack of meaning

Recovery is learning to transform what imprisons you.

**This course teaches:**
1. **Recognize your confinement** (what imprisons you?)
2. **Understand the pattern** (same 7-step process everywhere)
3. **Learn the method** (Lozoff's meditation + compassion)
4. **Choose your path** (which domain track fits your recovery?)

## Week 1: Recognition (Seeing Confinement)

### **Day 1: We're All Doing Time**
**Theme:** Lozoff's universal principle

**Morning (30 min):**
- Video: Bo Lozoff introduction (life, work, philosophy)
- Reading: Excerpts from "We're All Doing Time" (pages 1-20)
- Meditation: Guided introduction (10 min, Lozoff's voice if available)

**Daily Practice (15 min):**
- Sit quietly (15 min)
- Notice: What confines you? (don't judge, just observe)
- Journal: "What imprisons me?" (write freely, no editing)

**Evening Reflection (10 min):**
- Question: "Is this confinement permanent?"
- Question: "Who else might face similar confinement?"

**Group Circle (45 min, optional but encouraged):**
- Facilitator: Introduce Lozoff's principle
- Share: "What's one thing that confines you?" (pass, no obligation)
- Listen: Hear how others relate

**Resources:**
- Video: Bo Lozoff biography (YouTube)
- Text: "We're All Doing Time" Chapter 1
- Meditation: Guided sitting (mp3)
- Journal prompt

---

### **Day 2: Five Domains of Confinement**
**Theme:** The pattern repeats everywhere

**Morning (30 min):**
- Video: "Recursivity in nature" (how patterns repeat: fractals, systems, recovery)
- Introduction: 5-domain map
  - Personal (addiction, trauma)
  - ML (bias, overfitting)
  - Garden (soil depletion)
  - Criminal Justice (literal incarceration)
  - Community (systemic injustice)
- Key insight: **Same 7-step recovery process in all 5**

**Daily Practice (20 min):**
- Meditation: Scanning body/mind (20 min, finding tension/confinement)
- Journal: "How does my confinement show up physically/mentally/emotionally?"

**Learning Module (20 min):**
- Animation: How the 7-step pattern appears in each domain
- Read: Quick summaries (1-2 pages each) showing pattern in each domain

**Group Circle (45 min, optional):**
- Discuss: "I see confinement in my [domain]. How?"
- Listen: How others recognize patterns in their lives

**Resources:**
- Video: Recursivity patterns + 5-domain intro
- Interactive: 7-step pattern animation (click through each domain)
- Text: 1-pager on each domain's recovery process
- Meditation: Body scan + confinement awareness

---

### **Day 3: The 7-Step Pattern**
**Theme:** Recognize the structure

**Morning (40 min):**
- Video: Bo Lozoff's teachings on the 7 steps
  - Recognition (see clearly)
  - Belief (know change is possible)
  - Commitment (practice discipline)
  - Inventory (audit honestly)
  - Amends (make things right)
  - Integration (wholeness)
  - Service (teach others)

**Daily Practice (25 min):**
- Meditation: Loving-kindness (Lozoff's compassion practice, 15 min)
- Journal: "Where am I in the 7-step process right now?" (rate 1-7)

**Learning Module (25 min):**
- Case study 1: Personal recovery (addiction → healing, 7 steps)
- Case study 2: ML recovery (bias → fairness, same 7 steps)
- Case study 3: Soil recovery (depletion → regeneration, same 7 steps)

**Reflection:**
- "I see the pattern in all three"
- "What step am I at?"
- "What's next?"

**Resources:**
- Video: Lozoff's teachings on 7 steps
- Case studies: 3 examples showing same pattern
- Interactive: Where am I? Self-assessment tool

---

### **Day 4: Meditation as Recognition**
**Theme:** Lozoff's method for seeing clearly

**Morning (45 min):**
- Video: Lozoff on meditation (why contemplative practice enables recovery)
- Teaching: How to sit, breathe, observe without judgment
- Guided meditation: 20-min sitting (live or recorded)

**Daily Practice (30 min):**
- Meditation: 20-min sitting (same as morning, now solo)
- Journal: "What did I notice? What's beneath the confinement?"

**Learning Module (15 min):**
- Article: "Contemplative Practice & Transformation" (Lozoff-based)
- Highlight: How meditation reveals confinement + opens possibility

**Group Circle (60 min, strongly encouraged):**
- Sit together (20 min group meditation)
- Share: "What arose for me?"
- Discuss: How is meditation helping you see clearly?

**Resources:**
- Video: Lozoff teaching meditation
- Guided meditation: 20-min recording
- Article: Contemplative practice
- Circle guide for facilitators

---

### **Day 5: Compassion Practice**
**Theme:** Lozoff's "unconditional love" as recovery infrastructure

**Morning (45 min):**
- Video: Lozoff on compassion (why love transforms systems)
- Teaching: Loving-kindness practice (metta meditation)
- Guided practice: 20-min compassion meditation

**Daily Practice (30 min):**
- Meditation: Loving-kindness (20 min solo)
- Journal: "Whom do I need to extend compassion to? (Including myself)"

**Learning Module (20 min):**
- Article: "Love as Revolutionary Act" (Lozoff's philosophy)
- Highlight: How compassion bridges individual + systemic recovery

**Reflection:**
- "What happens when I approach my confinement with compassion?"
- "What changes?"

**Resources:**
- Video: Lozoff on compassion + love
- Guided practice: Loving-kindness meditation
- Article: Love as transformation
- Reflection prompts

---

### **Day 6: Your Confinement Story**
**Theme:** Making it personal

**Morning (30 min):**
- Reflection: Review 5 days of journaling
- Integration: "What story is emerging?"
- Writing: Draft your personal confinement story (500 words)

**Daily Practice (20 min):**
- Meditation: Grounding (10 min seated)
- Journaling: Complete your story (10 min)

**Writing Process (30 min):**
- Prompt: "I am imprisoned by [X] because [why]. It shows up as [how]. I've tried [what]. I'm ready to [next step]."
- Free write (no editing)
- Read aloud to yourself (hear your own story)

**Group Circle (90 min, strongly encouraged):**
- Share your story (5 min, if you choose to)
- Listen to others (hold space)
- Witness each other's confinement + readiness (compassion in action)
- Closing: "We're all doing time, and we're all ready to recover"

**Resources:**
- Story template
- Writing prompts
- Circle facilitation guide

---

### **Day 7: Integration & Choice**
**Theme:** From recognition to next steps

**Morning (45 min):**
- Video: Lozoff's legacy (50 years of teaching recovery + transformation)
- Reflection: Where are you in your recovery?
- Introduction: Choosing your track

**Daily Practice (25 min):**
- Meditation: Gratitude + intention (15 min)
- Journal: "What am I grateful for? What am I committing to?"

**Learning Module (20 min):**
- Overview of 5 domain tracks (short videos from each)
- Match: Which track aligns with your confinement + recovery?
- Options: Primary track + optional secondary tracks

**Individual Consultation (30-45 min):**
- Meet with counselor/guide
- Discuss: "What's your recovery path?"
- Recommend: Track(s) that fit
- Enroll: Choose your next course

**Celebration Circle (60-90 min, highly encouraged):**
- Recognize: Everyone who completed Week 1
- Share: Your commitment to recovery
- Commit: To your chosen track (public commitment = stronger accountability)
- Celebrate: Your readiness to transform

**Resources:**
- Video: Lozoff's legacy
- Track overviews (5 videos, 2 min each)
- Placement questionnaire
- Consultant guide
- Celebration ceremony outline

## Week 2: Practice & Commitment (Self-Paced OR Group Intensive)

### **Daily Structure (Repeated Days 8-14)**

**Morning Meditation (20 min):**
- Options: Sitting, loving-kindness, body scan, walking
- Practice: Choose one + commit to it daily
- Tracking: Log it (minutes, type, how you felt)

**Learning Module (20-30 min):**
- Video lectures from Lozoff
- Case studies from each domain
- Interactive: 7-step pattern in your life

**Reflection/Action (20 min):**
- Journal: Deepening your personal story
- Action: One small action toward your recovery
  - Personal: Attend meeting, call sponsor
  - ML: Audit a system for bias
  - Garden: Start a small plant, test soil
  - Justice: Research reentry programs
  - Community: Attend a community meeting

**Group Practice (30-60 min, 3x per week, optional but encouraged):**
- Meditation together
- Peer circles (share, listen, support)
- Progress check-in
- Q&A with facilitators

**Reflection (15 min):**
- Journal: What's shifting?
- Notice: Changes in how you see your confinement
- Celebrate: Small wins
