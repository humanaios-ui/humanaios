## Integration Points

### 1. Worker Onboarding → University Prerequisite

**Current flow:**
```
Worker applies → Authentication → Task assignment
```

**Enhanced flow:**
```
Worker applies → Authentication → "We're All Doing Time" (J0)
    ↓
Choose track: Personal Recovery OR Criminal Justice (if reentry)
    ↓
Deep recovery work (optional, self-paced)
    ↓
Task assignment (with recovery-informed dignity)
```

**Benefit:** Workers understand recovery as architectural principle, not just personal issue. Sets tone for dignified work relationship.

---

### 2. Reentry Workers → Criminal Justice Track

**HumanAIOS mission explicitly includes:** "20%+ of workforce comes from recovery community"

**University of Recursivity delivers:**
- **Criminal Justice track** (J1-J3): Reentry recovery + employment pathway
- **awesome-open-ag integration**: Reentry workers → agriculture jobs (proven pathway)
- **Mentorship**: Recovered workers mentor new reentrants
- **Dignity framework**: Recovery isn't charity; it's architecture

**Outcome:** HumanAIOS becomes the employment delivery mechanism for University graduates

---

### 3. AI Calibration (ACAT) → ML Recovery Track

**Current ACAT work:** Measures AI calibration + bias

**University addition:**
- **ML Recovery track**: Teaches AI systems to recognize + repair their own bias
- **Templates**: RecognitionTemplate, BeliefTemplate, AmendsTemplate (ML versions)
- **Integration**: Use ACAT findings to train ML recovery students
- **Feedback loop**: Students audit their own AI systems; contribute findings to ACAT

**Outcome:** Observatory becomes a teaching tool; AI learns from human recovery principles

---

### 4. Lasting Light Recovery → Peer Stories

**Lasting Light Recovery is:** Anonymity platform for humans to be seen safely

**University uses:**
- **Peer stories** (daily reading in J1.1, P1.1, M1.1, etc.)
- **Testimonials** (Belief phase: "See successful reentry/recovery")
- **Peer circles** (restorative justice groups)

**Data flow:**
```
Lasting Light Recovery → Anonymous stories database
                              ↓
                    University courses (read + reflect)
                              ↓
                    Cross-domain insights → ACAT findings
```

**Outcome:** Story library becomes the University's spiritual + practical foundation

---

### 5. awesome-open-ag → Garden Track + Employment

**amazing-open-ag is:** Open-source agriculture tools + community

**University + HumanAIOS + awesome-open-ag convergence:**

```
Recovery worker (reentry, in HumanAIOS)
    ↓
Takes University Garden track (learns soil recovery)
    ↓
Uses awesome-open-ag tools (horticulture, food sovereignty)
    ↓
Gets hired for agricultural work (HumanAIOS job placement)
    ↓
Mentors other reentry workers in gardening
    ↓
Contributes back to awesome-open-ag (soil data, seed varieties)
    ↓
Cycle continues: recovery → employment → teaching → ecosystem contribution
```

**Outcome:** Closed-loop recovery infrastructure connecting personal + economic + environmental justice

## The Marcus Test in HumanAIOS

**How the University validates the whole system:**

Marcus (fictional composite):
- **Year 1:** Gets hired by HumanAIOS (reentry worker)
  - Required: Take "We're All Doing Time" (J0)
  - Chooses: Criminal Justice track (reentry recovery)
  - Works: AI-human tasks + ongoing recovery
  
- **Year 2:** Deepens recovery
  - Enrolls: Personal Recovery track (addiction alongside incarceration)
  - Mentored: By recovered worker (HumanAIOS alum)
  - Contributing: Recovery stories to Lasting Light Recovery
  
- **Year 3:** Expands perspective
  - Takes: Garden track (discovers food justice angle)
  - Works: Some agriculture tasks (awesome-open-ag jobs)
  - Teaches: Others about soil recovery + human recovery parallels
  
- **Year 4:** Systems thinking
  - Studies: ML Recovery track (learns to audit algorithms)
  - Discovers: The AI that might bias against workers like him
  - Acts: Contributes to ACAT (helps calibrate fairness)
  
- **Year 5:** Leadership
  - Completes: Community track (systemic healing)
  - Becomes: Lozoff Facilitator (teaches J0)
  - Advocates: For justice reform + food sovereignty + fair AI

**Outcome:** One person demonstrates that recovery is universal, measurable, and transformative. Entire system validates.
