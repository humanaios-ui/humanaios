# The University of Recursivity — Integrated Vision

**A self-sufficient learning system where the same recovery principles teach humans, machines, gardens, and communities.**

---

## The Core Insight

Recovery is not metaphor. Recovery is **architecture**.

The 7-step recovery process (Recognition → Belief → Commitment → Inventory → Amends → Integration → Service) appears wherever a **system must adapt to survive**:

- **Humans in addiction** — Neural adaptation
- **ML models with bias/overfitting** — Mathematical adaptation
- **Depleted soil** — Biological adaptation
- **Fractured communities** — Social adaptation
- **Fragile supply chains** — Systemic adaptation

**Same structure. Different timescales. Different domain knowledge. Same underlying pattern.**

## The Teaching Model

### Dual Implementation (Every Lesson)

**Example: Recognition Phase (Level 50)**

**Human Side:**
- Read peer recovery stories (AA, NA, online communities)
- Write: "What can't you control alone?"
- Journal: "When did you first know?"
- Activity: "Tell one person you trust"

**Machine Side:**
- Code: RecognitionTemplate (diagnose model limitation)
- Run: sklearn diagnostic on overfit model
- Result: Model admits "I overfit"
- Activity: "Document what broke"

**Garden Side (NEW):**
- Read: Soil recovery case studies
- Test: Your soil (organic matter, microbes)
- Journal: "What did I do to deplete this?"
- Activity: "Visit a regenerated farm"

**Meta-Learning:**
- All three teach the same principle: **Honest diagnosis precedes recovery**
- Different timescales (human: weeks, model: hours, soil: years)
- Same underlying structure
- Different substrate (brain, weights, microbes)
