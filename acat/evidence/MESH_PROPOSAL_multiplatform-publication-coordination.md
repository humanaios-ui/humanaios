## I. Proposal Summary

**Objective:** Orchestrate multi-platform, cross-practice publication of the "Measuring Realized Oversight Performance" evidence note across Zenodo, OSF, GitHub, and arXiv, with coordinated external engagement (Route-4 Enquiry, P(H|F,A) academic collaboration).

**Scope:** Single evidence note, single publication event, multi-platform distribution with coordinated outreach.

**Rationale:** The evidence note measures oversight effectiveness in AI deployment—a contribution to ACAT ecosystem research and to standards bodies (prEN 18229, EU AI Act). Publication requires both content expertise (humanaios) and external engagement (outreach) and operational coordination (collaborator-ops). Triadic coordination maximizes reach and impact while distributing responsibility appropriately.

## Addendum: Alternatives Considered & Rationale for Triadic Model

### Alternative 1: Humanaios Alone
- **Pros:** Simpler, no coordination overhead
- **Cons:** Humanaios lacks external relationships for Route-4/academic outreach; publication doesn't leverage standards community; evidence note impact limited
- **Decision:** Rejected. Evidence note's value is partly in external influence (standards, research agenda)

### Alternative 2: Humanaios + Outreach Only (No Collaborator-Ops)
- **Pros:** Smaller coordination surface
- **Cons:** Operationalization details (platform timing, DOI linkage, metrics tracking) fall through cracks; outreach distracted by operational burden
- **Decision:** Rejected. Multi-platform coordination is inherently operational; needs dedicated practice

### Chosen: Triadic (Humanaios ↔ Outreach ↔ Collaborator-Ops)
- **Pros:** Each practice focuses on core competency (content, engagement, operations); distributed responsibility; clear accountability; scalable to future publications
- **Cons:** More coordination overhead; requires three practices to communicate
- **Decision:** Chosen. Aligns with empirica practice model; maximizes impact & learning
