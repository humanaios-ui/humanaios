## 4. Two general-purpose designs delivered this close

**Tri-source bug/feedback collection.** Three observers, one append-only ledger:
- **Machine** — self-reports its own defects and uncertainty: `window.onerror` capture, invariant self-check (WIP-limit breach, Human over-allocation, allocation-conservation/overstatement violations), and fidelity self-tagging (DEFECTIVE/DIVERGENT-VALID). The machine catching itself, as IC-031 self-audit did.
- **System** — runtime/telemetry independent of intent: landing events, blocked pulls, co-processor availability, onboarding instantiation. Non-judgmental, continuous.
- **User** — explicit Report affordance + severity; captures intent-translation mismatches — the collision-to-finding channel, instrumented.
Every entry is source-tagged, timestamped, append-only, and routes toward a finding. This operationalizes the mutual-observation premise: machine observes itself, system observes runtime, you observe output; any collision becomes a conserved finding, never discarded.

**Onboarding reverse-engineered from the product (global adoption, any intent).** The finished cockpit's schema, inverted into questions:
- Primary Intent ← "What are you trying to do?" (any domain)
- Active Work (WIP≤3) ← "What's active right now?"
- Harness caps ← "What can you marshal?" (your Human/Machine/Capital)
- Invariants ← four confirmations that install the disciplines (landing-over-volume, ratify-before-act, misreads-as-findings, tri-source observation)
The pilot data becomes a loadable *example*, not hardcoded. Any user's intent instantiates the same disciplined loop. Global adoption = the invariants are the universal scaffold; only the intent varies.
